Pillar 1 • 30% Weight

Quantification & Specificity

Verifies physical metrics (tCO2e, MWh, m³, %) rather than vague qualitative statements. Cross-referenced against GRI 305-1, GRI 305-2, and SASB TC-SC-110a.1.

Pillar 2 • 25% Weight

Trajectory & Baselines

Verifies defined base years, interim targets (2025, 2030), and annual milestones compliant with IFRS S2 para 33–36 & SBTi Corporate Standard.

Pillar 3 • 25% Weight

Scope & Materiality

Inspects full boundary coverage, specifically requiring Scope 3 supply chain disclosure when material. Aligned with TCFD & GHG Protocol Corporate Standard.

Pillar 4 • 20% Weight

Third-Party Assurance

Validates independent verification by accredited audit firms under ISAE 3000, ISO 14064-3, or AA1000AS, differentiating limited from reasonable assurance.

Project Mission & Problem Statement

Corporate sustainability disclosures frequently suffer from greenwashing—the selective disclosure of positive environmental actions while omitting material impacts, using vague unscientific claims, or announcing distant 2050 net-zero goals without interim roadmaps.

GreenWash-Screener is an autonomous multi-stage audit pipeline that parses corporate sustainability reports (PDFs), extracts empirical claims, and cross-examines them against a vector-indexed knowledge base of international sustainability frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD, ISSB, and EU Directive 2024/825).

The 4-Pillar Greenwashing Risk Index (GWRI)

GWRI = 0.30·P_quant + 0.25·P_traj + 0.25·P_scope + 0.20·P_assur
  • Low Risk (< 30): Substantiated with physical metrics, defined base years, Scope 3 inclusion, and verified by accredited auditors.
  • Grey Zone (30–70): Incomplete disclosures; vague target roadmaps, partial scope boundaries, or missing independent assurance.
  • High Risk (> 70): Unsubstantiated claims; generic superlative language ("eco-friendly", "clean pioneer") or cherry-picked distractions.
